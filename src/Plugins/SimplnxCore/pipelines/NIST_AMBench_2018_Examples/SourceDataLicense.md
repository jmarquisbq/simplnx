# Source data and reuse

Source: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), **Additive Manufacturing Benchmark Test Series (AM-Bench) 2018 Test Descriptions**, AMB2018-01 single-part CAD `AMB2018_01_Part.STL`.

- [Official benchmark description](https://www.nist.gov/ambench/amb2018-01-description)
- [Public data catalog and license declaration](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/additive-manufacturing-benchmark-test-series-am-bench-2018-test-descriptions)
- [NIST reuse terms](https://www.nist.gov/open/license)

The original STL is downloaded unchanged. It is nominal CAD used for both IN625 and 15-5PH benchmark builds; it contains no measured deformation, microstructure or material-property arrays. NIST's published dimensions establish millimeter coordinates. NIST is acknowledged as the source; the example is not a NIST endorsement or benchmark submission.

The catalog identifies NIST's open license. Its non-SRD data terms permit reuse, redistribution and derivative works with acknowledgement and identification of changes. U.S. government copyright treatment does not imply unrestricted public-domain status in every jurisdiction; retain the linked terms when redistributing.

The example creates derivative slices, a crop, one selected section, interior sample points and a figure of those native outputs. Those are example processing choices, not additional NIST measurements. No NIST figure is copied into this category. The raw asset is not committed or added to the application data package; packaging remains subject to review and approval.
